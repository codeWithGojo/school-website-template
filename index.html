Academic programme

A curriculum built to open two doors, not one

Students follow the Nigerian secondary curriculum through JSS1–JSS3, then choose a senior track aligned to WAEC/NECO and Cambridge IGCSE, so university options stay open both locally and abroad.

JSS1 – JSS3

Junior Secondary

A broad foundation across sciences, humanities, and vocational subjects, with continuous assessment used to guide senior-track placement.

  • Core Nigerian curriculum + Cambridge Lower Secondary
  • Compulsory French, ICT & Civic Education
  • Termly parent-teacher conferences
SSS1 – SSS3

Sciences Track

For students headed toward Medicine, Engineering, and applied sciences, with fully equipped physics, chemistry, and biology laboratories.

  • Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Further Maths
  • JAMB & WAEC-aligned practicals
  • Cambridge IGCSE science options
SSS1 – SSS3

Arts & Commercial

For students headed toward Law, Business, and the Humanities, built around writing, argument, and financial literacy.

  • Literature, Government, Economics, Accounting
  • Debate & Model UN society
  • Entrepreneurship & digital skills electives

Admissions 2026/2027

Four steps to a seat at Crown Heritage

  1. Submit the online application with your child's academic records.
  2. Register for the entrance examination (JSS1 & SSS1 intake).
  3. Attend the offer interview with a parent or guardian present.
  4. Accept your offer and complete registration before the session begins.

Start your application

Entrance exams hold on 24 October 2026 across all three campuses. Spaces are limited per class.
